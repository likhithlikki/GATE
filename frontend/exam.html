      Marking profile: GATE 2026 (source-reviewed reference only)

      Reference only: reviewed against the official GATE 2026 question paper pattern and answer keys pages. This is not an approved scoring profile. The zero score for an unanswered question is this site’s interpretation, not an official statement. NAT boundary inclusivity and response syntax/rounding remain unresolved. Only 2026 has a source-reviewed profile; every other year stays unverified and blocked.

      This lookup is informational only. It cannot approve a profile, enable scoring or start an online attempt.

      Policy status: draft, not owner-approved. Scoring and online attempts are blocked.

        Current policy and scoring blockers:

          Attempt policy, once the backend exists: no pause, resuming never extends the deadline, the exam service closes the attempt at the deadline and submits the responses already saved, one active attempt per mode, at most 10 starts per hour, and a 15-second technical grace only for a final submission already in flight. None of this is enforced today.

          Duration is fixed by the total marks of the paper: round(total marks × 10800 ÷ 65) seconds. See the help page. You do not set the time.
