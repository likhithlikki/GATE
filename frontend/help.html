Getting started

Open the practice page to see the five planned modes: year-wise papers, subject-wise tests, topic-wise practice, custom tests and full-length mock exams. Practice itself is not available yet, because the question bank has not been imported.

You will need an account to save results. See Accounts and signing in.

Accounts and signing in

Planned. You register with a username, your full name, a Gmail address and a password, or with Google. To register with a password you verify your Gmail address with a one-time code. The code expires and works only once.

If you forget your password, use password reset. A code is sent to the Gmail address on the account, and the same message is shown whether or not an account matches what you typed.

Only Gmail addresses are accepted. Sign in with Apple is not offered.

None of this is connected yet. The register and log in forms check what you type in your browser and then tell you they cannot send it anywhere.

How the test duration is set

You never choose the time. It is worked out from the total marks of the questions in your test:

Duration = total marks ÷ 65 × 180 minutes

The result is rounded to the nearest second. A 65-mark test lasts 180 minutes. Duration depends on the marks, not on the number of questions.

How full-length mock exams are composed and timed is still to be confirmed. The timer on the server is the one that counts. The clock on your screen is only a display.

Question types and marking

MCQ has one correct option. MSQ can have more than one correct option. NAT asks you to enter a number.

[Marking rules for each type, including any negative marking, partial credit and the tolerance for NAT answers, are to be confirmed and will be shown before every test starts.]

The exam window

Planned. The exam window shows a countdown timer, a question palette, buttons to move between questions, clear a response or mark it for review, and a scientific calculator. Answers are saved as you go, so an interrupted attempt can be resumed against the same deadline. You cannot pause a test.

In the palette, green means answered, red means not answered, purple means marked for review and grey means not visited.

Results, history and bookmarks

Planned. After you submit, you see your score and which questions you got right. Correct answers and explanations are shown only after you submit, and only for the questions in that attempt.

Your dashboard, exam history and bookmarks are described on their own pages. Your profile holds your account details.

If you find a mistake in a question, answer or score, tell us through the contact page.

What works today

Today the site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. The question bank, accounts, practice and exams are not connected. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use drafts for what is planned, and the security page for how scoring and answer keys are meant to be handled.
