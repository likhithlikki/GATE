Current status

Today this site consists of static pages. The registration, login, password reset, practice and exam features are not connected, and the forms do not send information anywhere. Nothing described below as planned is operating yet.

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, under its own policies, which we do not control.

Account information and Gmail verification

Planned. When registration is switched on, we expect to collect a username, your full name and your Gmail address. If you sign in with Google, we expect to receive your Google account identifier, name and email address from Google.

To verify a Gmail address, we plan to send a one-time code by email. The code is meant to expire and to work only once.

We do not intend to store passwords in readable form. The method used to protect them has not been finalised.

We also expect to keep basic session information, such as a session token and its expiry, so that you stay logged in on your device.

Practice attempts, answers, scores, bookmarks and progress

Planned. When practice and exams are active, we expect to record the tests you start, your answers, timing, submission, scores and results, the questions you bookmark, and your progress. This is what powers your history and dashboard.

How we plan to use information

We plan to use your information to create and secure your account, verify your email address, run and score your tests, show your history and progress, respond to support requests, and detect abuse such as repeated automated requests or attempts to tamper with scores.

[Sharing, advertising and analytics practices to be confirmed by the site owner and described here before launch.]

Where data is planned to be stored

The intended design stores data in Google Sheets and processes it with Google Apps Script, both provided by Google. If this design changes, this policy will be updated.

Google Sheets is a spreadsheet, not a purpose-built database. It has usage limits and weaker guarantees than a dedicated database service, and we have not yet set up or verified backups.

Third-party services and Google sign-in

Active today: GitHub Pages hosting only.

Planned: Google sign-in, Google Sheets and Google Apps Script, and Gmail for sending one-time codes. These services are governed by Google’s own terms and privacy policy. Sign in with Apple is not offered.

We have not yet verified what additional information Google may collect when you use these features.

Access controls and security limits

Planned. The design is for the server to check who you are on every request, to let you see only your own data, to keep answer keys away from your browser during an exam, and to score tests on the server. Access for administrators is intended to be restricted to a small number of authorised people. These controls are not yet built or tested.

No online service can be made completely secure. We cannot promise that your information will never be accessed, lost or changed without authorisation.
