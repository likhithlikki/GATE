See the full test before you start

You never set the timer yourself. It follows from the marks in your test.

Duration = total marks ÷ 65 × 180 minutes

A 65-mark test gets 180 minutes. Other totals are scaled and rounded to the nearest second.

  1. Pick your questions. Choose a mode and apply filters.
  2. Review the summary. It shows the matching question count, selected questions, total marks, question types, marking rules and duration.
  3. Take the test. The countdown starts when you confirm.
  4. Check your results. Your attempt is saved to your history.
